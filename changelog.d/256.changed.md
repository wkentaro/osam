SAM3 runs the sam3-onnx v0.7.0 models, in float16 on GPUs, and keeps image features on the GPU on CUDA. Image encoding takes 197 ms on an RTX 3080 Ti with CUDA and 1.2 s on an Apple M5 with CoreML, excluding text decoding, transport and output serialization. The measured first CoreML load takes 5 s with no existing compilation cache.
