On macOS with onnxruntime 1.24 or later, SAM3 runs on CoreML unless a device or `OSAM_ONNX_PROVIDERS` is given. On an Apple M5, image encoding takes 1.2 s on CoreML and 2.4 s on WebGPU, excluding text decoding, transport and output serialization. CoreML writes a 1.8 GB compilation cache on the first load.
