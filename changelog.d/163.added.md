`osam.Client` sends a repeated image by its reference, and encodes images at PNG level 1, which is 4 times faster; `/api/segment` returns the image's reference, `"sha256:<hex>"`, in `image`, which a later request can send as `image` in place of the file, and answers 412 with `"code": "missing_images"` and the references in `images` when it no longer has that image.
