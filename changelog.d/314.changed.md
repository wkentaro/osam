SAM, EfficientSAM, SAM2 and SAM3 draw a mask the same way, from the model's low-resolution logits sampled at each pixel center. SAM's mask edge moves on images whose long side is not 1024. SAM3's text masks move where they were drawn from probabilities, and on an image under 288 px a side SAM3 no longer averages the logits under each pixel, so a part of a mask thinner than a pixel can drop out.
